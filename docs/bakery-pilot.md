# Bakery two-order pilot

Run a two-week pilot with 3–5 independent bakery owners. Each participant uses Event Profit Watch for two **real** custom orders, from pricing and quoting to payment and delivery. The app now has a guide in **Bakery orders → Two-order pilot** and an in-app feedback form. The owner can review submissions at `/pilot-feedback` after signing in with the configured pilot admin account.

## Invite and set up

Send this individually to owners you already know. Do not create test orders in their accounts or collect customer details in a separate spreadsheet.

> I'm testing a small web app that helps custom bakeries price orders, track actual costs and customer payments, and see profit before delivery. Would you be willing to use it for two real orders over the next two weeks? It is free during this pilot. You can stop anytime. Feedback you send in the app is visible to me with your sign-in email and the number of orders you marked delivered; I cannot view your order details or finances. I would value 15 minutes of honest feedback at the end.

Give each person the [live beta](https://event-profit-watch.kushalkachari993.chatgpt.site/). Ask them to sign in, open **Bakery orders**, and set their business profile before sharing a quote. Avoid asking for passwords or payment details.

## What participants do

1. Add an order with selling price and estimated ingredients, labour, packaging, and delivery costs.
2. Preview the customer quote, then share it using **Open WhatsApp**, **Copy message**, or **Print / Save PDF**. WhatsApp opens a draft that the owner reviews and sends. The app does not verify that a quote was sent.
3. Record actual costs and customer payments as they occur. Move the order to **Delivered** when complete.
4. Repeat with a second real order. Use **Give feedback** whenever a step is confusing or slow.

The pilot progress count is based only on orders marked **Delivered**, including archived ones. It is a guide, not an audit of all steps.

## Owner check-ins and decision

On day 1, ask participants to attempt their first order without a walkthrough. On days 3–5, ask where they got stuck and invite an in-app note. At the end of week 2, hold a 15-minute call with each owner. Ask what they used instead, which step took longest, whether they would use the app again, and what one change would make it useful enough to keep using.

Review `/pilot-feedback` daily. It shows the latest 500 notes with step, ease score, sign-in email, and number of delivered orders at submission. Count participants who complete two orders, identify the most repeated blocker, and choose one next feature from observed behavior. Do not treat the ease score alone as proof of demand.

Suggested pilot success signal: at least 3 owners try one real order, at least 2 complete two orders, and at least 2 say they would keep using it without prompting. These are learning targets, not a claim that the product is validated.
